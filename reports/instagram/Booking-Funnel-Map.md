## What Each Stage Needs From Your Content

| Funnel Stage | What Drives It | Content That Works |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Reach → Profile Visit | Curiosity, credibility | Process videos, latte art transformations, "did you know" coffee facts, behind-the-scenes |
| Profile Visit → Link Click | Clear bio, strong CTA | Bio: what you do + who it's for + "Book a session ↓". Keep it short. |
| Link Click → Booking | Easy booking, trust | Direct Calendly link (no extra steps). Social proof helps. |
| Booking → Deposit | Fast follow-up | Send deposit link within 1 hour of booking notification |
| Session → Repeat | Great experience + follow-up | Post-session DM within 48hrs, specific progress noted, bundle offer |

## The One Number That Predicts Bookings Best

**Saves per post.**

When someone saves a post, it means:
- They found it valuable enough to come back to
- The algorithm shows it to more people
- They are a warm prospect (high intent)

A post with 30 saves from 300 people reached (10% save rate) will get you more bookings than a post with 500 likes and 0 saves.

**Track saves every week. Optimise for saves.**
