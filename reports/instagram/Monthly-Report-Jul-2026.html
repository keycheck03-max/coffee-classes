Keep posting Reels — Instagram proved 2 more Reels = +93% views This month you posted 2 more Reels than the previous month. Instagram's own analysis confirmed that alone drove 93% more views. Fix the bio — this is costing you 3–5 bookings per month 265 people visited your profile, only 18 tapped the link (6.8%). The target is 15%. Your bio needs to answer: "Who is this for, what will I get, and where do I go?" Try: "Private barista training in Cairns · Latte art + espresso fundamentals · 1-on-1 · Book below ↓". Zero comments in 30 days — no conversations = no community signal The algorithm treats comments as proof that content matters. You have 0. 3 saves in 30 days — saves are how you grow reach without posting A save tells Instagram to keep showing your content to new people. 3 saves in a month is almost nothing. Create one "reference" post per week — a checklist, a common mistake breakdown, a comparison. People save things they plan to return to.
