# How To Pull Your Instagram Numbers
### Make Coffee With Love — Step-by-step, every Monday morning

---

## Before You Start

You need a **Professional (Creator) account** on Instagram.  
If you're still on a personal account: Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account → Creator.

Everything below pulls from **Instagram's built-in Insights** — no third-party tools needed.

---

## WHERE TO FIND EACH NUMBER

---

### Followers & Growth
**Path:** Profile → tap the bar chart icon (top right) → Overview → Followers  
**What to record:**
- Total followers (current number on your profile)
- The "+ / −" change shown in the Followers tile

---

### Accounts Reached & Impressions
**Path:** Professional Dashboard → Overview → select "Last 7 days" → Accounts Reached  
**What to record:**
- "Accounts reached" = unique people who saw your content (use this for % calculations)
- "Impressions" = total views including repeat views (useful for trending but don't use for rates)

---

### Profile Visits
**Path:** Professional Dashboard → Overview → Profile Activity → Profile Visits  
**What to record:** The number shown under "Profile visits"

---

### Bio Link Clicks (Website Taps)
**Path:** Professional Dashboard → Overview → Profile Activity → Website Taps  
**What to record:** The number shown under "Website taps"  
> If you use Linktree or a custom link, also check that tool's dashboard for clicks per destination (e.g. how many went to Calendly vs other links).

---

### Likes, Comments, Saves, Shares
**Path:** Professional Dashboard → Content You Shared → tap any post → View Insights  
For a weekly total:  
- Go to each post published that week
- Add up likes, comments, saves, shares across all posts  
**OR** use the Overview → Interactions number for total weekly interactions.

---

### Stories Performance
**Path:** Go to your Stories archive → tap a story from this week → swipe up  
You'll see:
- Views (accounts reached)
- Replies
- Link sticker taps (if you added a link)
- Forward/back taps
> Note: Story insights disappear after 14 days. Check these within the week.

---

### Calendly Page Views & Bookings
**Path:** calendly.com → your event type → click the "..." → Analytics  
**What to record:**
- "Views" = page views (people who landed on your booking page)
- "Bookings" = people who actually picked a time
> If you have two event types (Barista Fundamentals + Latte Art), check both and add them together.

---

### Stripe Deposits
**Path:** dashboard.stripe.com → Payments → filter by "This week"  
**What to record:**
- Count of successful payments = confirmed deposits = real bookings
- Note any failed or refunded payments

---

### Session Count (New vs Returning)
**Track this manually** — it takes 30 seconds.  
Each week, count:
- How many sessions did you run?
- How many were with someone you've trained before?
> Tip: Keep a simple note in your phone with client names and session dates. After 10+ sessions you'll start to see the recurring rate clearly.

---

## THE 10-MINUTE MONDAY RITUAL

1. Open the Weekly Report Template
2. Open Instagram on your phone
3. Pull numbers from Professional Dashboard (5 minutes)
4. Open Calendly + Stripe on your laptop (2 minutes)
5. Fill in the Scorecard and write 2 action items (3 minutes)
6. Copy numbers into Metrics-Tracker.csv (1 minute — do this monthly if weekly feels like too much)

**Total: ~10 minutes.**

That's it. Do it every Monday before you post anything new.

## UNDERSTANDING YOUR NUMBERS — WHAT'S NORMAL

### Early stage (0–500 followers)
| Metric | Acceptable | Good | Excellent |
|--------|-----------|------|-----------|
| Engagement rate | 4–6% | 6–10% | 10%+ |
| Save rate | 1% | 2–3% | 4%+ |
| Profile visit rate | 3% | 5% | 8%+ |
| Link click rate | 8% | 15% | 25%+ |
| Booking conversion | 10% | 20% | 35%+ |

> With a niche service business like coffee training, lower reach + higher engagement + higher conversion is the goal. You don't need 10,000 followers. You need 300 people who are genuinely interested.

### The math that matters most
**To get 1 booking per week, you need approximately:**
- 500 accounts reached
- → 25 profile visits (5% visit rate)
- → 4 bio link clicks (15% link click rate)
- → 1 booking (25% booking conversion)

**Once you hit 1,000 accounts reached per week, you should be getting 2–3 bookings per week from Instagram alone.**

## RED FLAGS TO WATCH FOR

| Signal | What it means | What to do |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| High reach, low profile visits | Content isn't creating curiosity | Improve hooks — make people want to know who's behind the post |
| High profile visits, low link clicks | Bio or link isn't compelling | Rewrite your bio and link destination |
| High link clicks, low bookings | Calendly page isn't converting | Simplify booking page, reduce steps |
| High bookings, low recurring | Post-session follow-up is weak | Improve your follow-up DM (see Booking Scripts) |
| Good engagement, zero bookings | Wrong audience or unclear CTA | Add clear CTAs, check your content is reaching local people |
