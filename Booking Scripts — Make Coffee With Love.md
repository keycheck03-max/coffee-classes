# Booking Scripts
### Make Coffee With Love — Copy-Paste DM & Email Templates

---

> **How to use this:** Copy the script, fill in the [ ] brackets, and send.
> Save these in your phone notes so you can paste them instantly.

## 1. WHEN SOMEONE ASKS ABOUT TRAINING

*Use when someone DMs "are you taking bookings?" or "how does it work?"*

---

**Short version (Instagram DM):**
```
Hey! Yes, totally 👋

I run two types of 1-on-1 coffee sessions:

☕ Barista Fundamentals (2.5hrs) — full beginner journey, extraction to latte art
🎨 Latte Art 1-on-1 (2.5hrs) — for people who already make coffee and want to level up their pours

Both are $180. I take a $20 deposit to lock in the time.

Which one sounds more like you?
```

---

**After they tell you which class:**
```
Perfect 👌 Here's the booking link for the [Barista Fundamentals / Latte Art] session:
[YOUR CALENDLY LINK FOR THAT CLASS]

Pick a time that works and I'll send over the deposit link to lock it in.
```

---

**Longer version (if they asked for details):**
```
Hey [Name]! Great to hear from you 👋

I run two types of sessions:

☕ BARISTA FUNDAMENTALS (2.5hrs — $180)
For complete beginners. We go through everything from scratch:
• How to pull a proper espresso
• Steaming milk to the right texture
• Your first latte art pours
Great if you've never made coffee before or want to start from the basics.

🎨 LATTE ART 1-on-1 (2.5hrs — $180)
For people who already make coffee but want to improve their latte art.
We focus on diagnosing what's off with your milk and breaking down the pour step by step.

Both sessions: just you and me, all equipment provided, $20 deposit to secure.

Sessions run Tue / Thu / Sat.

Which one sounds right for you? 😊
```

```
⏰ Time: [Start Time] – [End Time]
```

## 6. WHEN SOMEONE TRIES TO BOOK WITHOUT PAYING

*If someone chooses a time but doesn't pay the deposit after 24–48 hours.*

---

```
I can only hold the spot for [24/48] hours before opening it up again.

If you're still keen, here's the deposit link:
[YOUR STRIPE LINK]

And if the timing doesn't work anymore, no worries — you can always grab a new slot later:
[YOUR CALENDLY LINK]

Let me know either way 😊
```

## QUICK RULES (Print this and put it somewhere visible)

```
✅ DO:
- Send the payment link the same day they choose a time
- Confirm within minutes of receiving payment
- Send a reminder the day before
- Follow up after the session

❌ DON'T:
- Hold spots without deposit
- Chase people who haven't paid — send one reminder, then let it go
- Be vague about time, location, or payment
- Apologise for charging what you're worth
```
