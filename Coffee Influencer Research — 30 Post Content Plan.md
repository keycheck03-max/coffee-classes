## Influencer Breakdowns + Their 3 Best Post Ideas

---

### 1. James Hoffmann (@jameshoffmann)
**Why he works:** World Barista Champion 2007. His "science meets storytelling" approach makes complex coffee concepts feel accessible. His gear comparison videos shift product sales overnight. Audience = home enthusiasts who want to spend smart on equipment.

**Best post types:**
- "Ultimate guide to X" long-form videos
- Myth-busting content
- Side-by-side gear tests

**His 3 post concepts adapted for Make Coffee With Love:**

> **Post 1** — *"The truth about expensive espresso machines"*
> Carousel: "You bought the machine. Now what?" — breaks down why technique (not gear) is the #1 variable. Ends with CTA: "This is exactly what we cover in your first session with me."

> **Post 2** — *"5 espresso myths I debunk in every session"*
> Carousel: myth vs. reality format (e.g. "tamping pressure matters most" — FALSE). Builds authority, creates conversation in comments.

> **Post 3** — *"What extraction actually means — and why it changes everything"*
> Reel: 30–60 second visual explanation of under vs. over extraction. Positions Jimmy as the expert who can teach this 1-on-1.

### 2. Lance Hedrick (@lancehedrick)
**Why he works:** 2x CoffeeFest Latte Art World Champion, 500k+ YouTube. Known for bridging the gap between professional barista training and home use. His content proves you can get competition-level skills without a café job.

**Best post types:**
- Step-by-step latte art tutorials
- "What pros do vs. what home baristas do" comparisons
- Equipment deep dives for serious home baristas

**His 3 post concepts adapted for Make Coffee With Love:**

> **Post 4** — *"Learn the rosetta in 4 steps — what I teach every student"*
> Reel: POV over-the-shoulder tutorial, 45 seconds. Break the pour into 4 clear movements. CTA: "Want me to guide you through this live? Sessions open now."

> **Post 5** — *"Why home baristas plateau — and the one fix that changes everything"*
> Carousel: The #1 plateau is milk texture, not the pour. 5 slides showing what flat milk looks like vs. microfoam. Ends with "this is fixable in one session."

> **Post 6** — *"What competition baristas do that you can copy at home"*
> Carousel: 5 competition habits (pre-heat cups, consistent dosing, timing shots, tasting, journaling) — reframed as things Jimmy teaches in Barista Fundamentals.

### 3. Morgan Eckroth (@morgandrinkscoffee)
**Why she works:** 2022 US Barista Champion + 7M combined followers. Comedy-driven content makes barista life relatable. Bridges café professionals and general audiences. Her format: 30-second relatable skits that both casual viewers and serious baristas love.

**Best post types:**
- Comedy skits about barista life
- Behind-the-scenes café content
- "Types of customers / baristas" relatable posts

**Her 3 post concepts adapted for Make Coffee With Love:**

> **Post 7** — *"POV: It's your first time in a coffee training session with me"*
> Reel: Comedy/warm tone. Show the nervous first pull, the mess, the improvement — ending on that first proud smile. Real, human, and shareable.

> **Post 8** — *"Things people say before their first barista job (vs. what actually happens)"*
> Carousel or Reel: "I'll be fine, I've made coffee at home for years" → reality cuts. Ends: "Training with me first changes this outcome."

> **Post 9** — *"The 3 types of students who book with me — which one are you?"*
> Carousel: Type 1 = wants a café job, Type 2 = just bought a $3,000 machine, Type 3 = wants to impress people. Funny, relatable, and covers both target audiences.

### 4. Dritan Alsela (@dritanalsela)
**Why he works:** 1M+ Instagram followers, German barista legend, owns cafés, roastery, and barista school. Famous for "Respect the beans!" philosophy and lightning-speed latte art. His content = craft pride + aspirational skill.

**Best post types:**
- Latte art speed pour Reels
- Craft philosophy content ("why coffee is an art")
- Barista school and training highlights

**His 3 post concepts adapted for Make Coffee With Love:**

> **Post 11** — *"Coffee is a conversation between you and the machine"*

> **Post 12** — *"I broke latte art down to 4 steps — so anyone can start"*
> Tutorial Reel: 60 seconds, 4 clear actions — jug angle, stretch, texture, pour height. Branded: "What we cover in your Latte Art 1-on-1 session."

### 5. Barista Hustle / Matt Perger (@baristahustle)
**Why he works:** Australian world champion (World Brewers Cup 2012). Built the leading online barista training platform. Content = highly educational, extraction-focused, trusted by serious baristas globally. Audience = people who want to go deep on coffee science.

**Best post types:**
- Extraction science explanations
- Professional barista course content previews
- Coffee control chart education

**His 3 post concepts adapted for Make Coffee With Love:**

> **Post 13** — *"The coffee control chart — every barista should know this"*
> Carousel: Visual breakdown of the extraction chart (strength vs. extraction). Teach the quadrants. Position Jimmy as the trainer who explains this in plain English, in person.

> **Post 14** — *"You're probably over-extracting — here's how to tell in 10 seconds"*
> Reel: Quick taste test method + visual cues. Fast, educational, shareable. Ends: "We fix this in your first session together."

> **Post 15** — *"What professional barista training actually teaches you (that YouTube can't)"*
> Carousel: 5 things you can only learn hands-on — real-time feedback, dialling in your specific equipment, tasting under guidance, building muscle memory. Positions 1-on-1 training as irreplaceable.

## Posting Strategy Notes

**Content split (30 posts):**
- 14 posts → targeting aspiring baristas wanting café jobs
- 12 posts → targeting high-end home espresso enthusiasts
- 4 posts → dual-audience (appeal to both equally)

**Format split:**
- 18 Reels (short-form video — highest reach on Instagram)
- 12 Carousels (educational/saves — highest saves and DMs)

**Suggested posting rhythm:**
- 3 posts per week = 10 weeks of content
- Alternate: Reel → Carousel → Reel each week
- Pin the strongest authority post (Post 15 or Post 28) to profile top

**CTA rotation (don't use the same one every time):**
- "Sessions open — link in bio"
- "DM me 'coffee' to find out more"
- "Which type are you? Drop it below"
- "Save this for when you're ready to level up"
- "Book your session — Tue / Thu / Sat"
