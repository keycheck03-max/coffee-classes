FAQ

Questions? Covered.

Do I need my own equipment?
No — all equipment is provided. Just bring yourself and wear something comfortable. We'll be hands-on the whole session.
What if I'm a complete beginner?
Perfect. We start from where you are. I'll ask about your experience level when you book so we can make the most of our 2.5 hours together.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes — please give at least 48 hours notice to reschedule. The deposit is non-refundable for last-minute cancellations (less than 24 hours notice).
Why the $20 deposit?
It keeps things professional. A deposit confirms you're serious and means I can hold the time just for you. The remaining balance is paid on the day — cash or card.
What is the Full Barista Programme?
It's a complete 6-hour training split into two 3-hour sessions — available on weekends or across two different days. Session 1 covers all the fundamentals. Session 2 goes deeper into bar workflow, the full café drink menu, mise en place, drink sequencing, and a real rush simulation. By the end, you'll be ready to work a café bar.
Where is the session held?
Location details are sent in your confirmation after booking. Sessions are held in Cairns — specific location shared once you're booked in.
